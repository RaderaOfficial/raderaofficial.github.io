Booking Info

Radera delivers high-energy sets ranging from Rawstyle to Uptempo, backed by hit releases like “ON AND ON AND ON”, “PSYKOPAT (Radera Remix)”, and “MY SYSTEM”.

He has brought his signature sound to stages across Europe—including The Netherlands, Germany, Sweden, Norway, Denmark, Finland, Estonia, and Poland—performing at major events such as Sana Duri, One Vision Festival, DNA OUTDOOR, Summerburst, and Owl Festival. Beyond festivals, Radera frequently takes over full club nights, spanning Classic Hardstyle, Rawstyle, Hardcore, and Uptempo.

With an extensive catalog, he adapts seamlessly to any time slot or stage. Radera offers 1-hour solo sets, an explosive 30-minute LIVE ACT, or full 4–5 hour solo club nights. His sets are also known for guest appearances like MIAZ, Direktören, and Emelie.
